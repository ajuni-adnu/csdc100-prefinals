What is Generative AI?

Among many types of AI, GenAI is one that creates new content based on pre-existing materials online and online-input (Meriam Webster, n.d). The content varies which ranges from “text, images, audio, video, etc.”. Although GenAI is mostly famous for text and video use.

How does Generative AI work?

Human Input
Generative AI is possible because of massive input that neural networks processes (Nvidia, n.d). Given a certain label, the AI analyze, predict and classify each respective feature. In other words, the input mentioned earlier give the AI the basic understanding of how subject A, B, C through their texture, color, shape, patterns, etc. They then recreate new content given the existing data (Amazon AWS, n.d).

What are the strengths of Generative AI?

Human Input
According to the University of Leeds, these are the strengths of GenAI:

  • Diverse output, being to exposed to billions of simulations or view points allow the AI to create new and unique content.

  • Increased productivity or efficiency, manual and repetitive tasks are lessened with the use of AI and as a result, individuals can focus on the real work that matters.

  • Personalised experience, the Ai adapts to the user on how they act or how the user wants the AI to act. With this, if you want a desired and specific result: you simply ask for it.

AI Input

What are the weakness of Generative AI?

Human Input
According to the University of Leeds, these are the strengths of GenAI:

  • Inaccuracy, Gen AI as of now is known to confidently state incorrect facts that seem trustworthy despite having no valid source or basis (Southern New Hampshire University, n.d), this is called AI hallucinations (Google Cloud, n.d). Furthermore, the AI cannot differentiate varying opinions from the given user-input data to actual facts which may cause the AI to form biases.

  • Lack of Semantic Understanding: As mentioned earlier, AI does not fully understand what it's saying in a semantic sense: this is called Stochastic parroting (Moveworks, n.d) One AI that will most likely conquer this is: AGI.

  • Copyright issues, GenAI feeds on any content given such as intellectual property without permission of the original owners for its training dataset. Lack of authoritative legislation and copyright issues within GenAI has also been present ever since its existence. (University of Leeds, n.d)
