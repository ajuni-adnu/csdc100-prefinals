Analogy on the use of AI in Dev Work

You are organizing your wedding, the documentation team is asking 40k for the wedding package. They will use an Iphone 16 pro max to cover the event and CapCut for the same day edit video. The documentation team has 2 years of experience and has an above average portfolio. Would you agree to that deal? Explain your answer and justify the integrity of their work. Reflect on the integrity, creativity, skill level of the “vibe” coders.
It depends to be honest, if I like their portfolio I would agree but knowing that I haven't seen it yet: personally I would say no, and that's because I have an intermediate experience when it comes to editing videos. But even if I didn't, P40k is an overkill of a budget for something that uses Capcut. For a P40k budget, I would've expected something better like Final Cut Pro, Adobe Premiere, or Davinci Resolve. Although the Pro Max "might" be justifiable, it's possible to rent a DSLR camera for cheaper, which has BETTER camera quality than the Pro Max and you'll still have money to spend with the P40k budget. Might as well rent out a jack of all trade's service than the "documentation team", the jack-of-all-trades would've charged less and still have better quality. Not to disrespect the “team” or any vibe coders, but their service would only be reasonable and justified if their quality of work actually demands or matches that P40k budget, if it doesn’t: I’m not taking the offer, I might as well do it myself.
